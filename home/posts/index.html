title = Phil Eaton ---

Hey! I'm Phil (he/him). I was a software development manager at Oracle, I was a cofounder and led marketing at TigerBeetle, I was a developer working on Postgres products at EnterpriseDB. Today I'm bootstrapping [The Consensus](https://theconsensus.dev) to provide research and analysis for experienced programmers.

I am a college dropout (not by choice; stay in school!) and for the last 10 years I've chased my way down the software stack starting from humble beginnings with the venerable jQuery and PHP.

I try to practice [healthy obsession](https://notes.eatonphil.com/2024-08-24-obsession.html).

To that end, I run an online [Software Internals Book Club](https://eatonphil.com/bookclub.html) (3k+ members), run [/r/databasedevelopment](https://www.reddit.com/r/databasedevelopment/) (10k+ members), and I run a [Software Internals Discord](https://eatonphil.com/discord.html) (9k+ members) that has, among other things, become a home for database and distributed systems developers.

I keep [cooking new dishes](https://www.instagram.com/eatonphilz), love to [read](https://www.goodreads.com/eatonphil), started [running](https://www.strava.com/athletes/eatonphil), and occasionally [play](https://www.youtube.com/@phil_at_home) music.

My [writing](https://notes.eatonphil.com), which in 2026 passed 1.5 million total views, has been cited in the book [Writing for Developers](https://www.manning.com/books/writing-for-developers).

I hope you connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eatonphil/) (11k+ followers), [Twitter](https://x.com/eatonphil) (28k+ followers), [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/eatonphil.bsky.social) (6k+ followers), my [mailing list](https://eatonphil.com/subscribe.html) (3k+ subscribers), or over [email](mailto:phil@eatonphil.com).
